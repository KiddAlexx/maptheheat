What is MapTheHeat?

MapTheHeat is a community-driven platform for discovering and sharing venues that serve spicy food. Members browse an interactive map, read community heat ratings and reviews, and contribute their own discoveries so other spice-lovers can find the best places to eat.

What you can do

  • Browse an interactive map or list to find restaurants and shops serving spicy food in cities around the world.
  • Read honest heat ratings and quality scores left by other members of the community.
  • Submit new venues, upload photos, and write your own reviews with heat ratings.
  • Save your favourite venues and manage your profile.

Signing in and how we use your data

MapTheHeat lets you sign in to create an account, which is required to save favourites, submit venues, upload photos, and write reviews. You can sign in with Google, in which case we only access your name and email address to create and secure your account. We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google services, and we never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

For full details on what we collect and your rights under GDPR, please read our Privacy Policy.
